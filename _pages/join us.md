**For PhD Students**

​I am looking for PhD students to join the group. **Note: Students who have qualified CSIR/ UGC JRF can directly appear for a walk-in interview at BITS Pilani. You can get in touch with me at anish.rao@pilani.bits-pilani.ac.in** Others will have to follow the BITS Pilani admission process by applying and qualifying the BITS Pilani PhD entrance exam. 

​​**For Postdocs**

I am looking for candidates interested in joining the group as postdocs, and willing to apply for NPDF to secure the postdoc funding. 
​​​
**For Undergraduates and Master students**
​​​
For BITS first degree and higher degree students, you can directly get in touch with me for the possibility of a semester-long project or a thesis.
For students outside BITS, there are summer internship opportunities and you can apply through those.
